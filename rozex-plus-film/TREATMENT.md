# Rozex Plus — "ليش ترجع؟" · Treatment

Style: **Tablet Sketch Explainer** (`styles/ipad-sketch`) · Length **20 s** · **1080×1920 vertical** (Instagram Reel) · 24 fps
No voice-over, no subtitles. All words are pencil lettering on the page, in **Iraqi Arabic**; product and ingredient names in English.
Audience: the public (end customers 18–34, women and men), Iraq.

---

## 1. Three candidate structures

| | Structure | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| A | **Question → the pore → four actives, one colour change each → the product.** An empty page with the customer's question; a pore drawn in cross-section; each active fixes one thing by re-registering one colour block; pinch out to the whole page with the tube and the title. | **Chosen.** Hook in 3 s, one clear turn, and the medium's own move (re-register a block) carries the science. |
| B | Before / after: the same face drawn twice, spotted then clear. | Rejected: reads as a before/after result promise, which the brand's compliance rules forbid. |
| C | The tube taken apart layer by layer ("holes as layers"), each layer one active. | Rejected: product-first, weaker hook for the public; better for a pharmacist piece. |

**Logline:** a pimple that keeps coming back is drawn as what it is (a blocked, oily, inflamed pore) and Rozex Plus's four actives redraw it, one colour at a time.

**Arc:** setup (the question, the blocked pore) → turn (four actives, four colour changes; the pore goes from red to calm) → ending (pinch out: the whole page, the tube, the title, how to use, how to order).
