# TREATMENT — Dermavia Dark Spots Cream · Scratchboard Long Take

**Format:** Instagram Reel, 1080×1920 (9:16), 30 fps, 26 s · on-screen text in Iraqi Arabic · music + foley, no voice
**Style:** Scratchboard Long Take (`printed-long-take`), ink `#16171b`, paper `#ede5cd`, one red `#c4332b`, warm yellow `#dfa443` for sunlight only
**The one red:** a printmaker's seal. It stamps ❌ on the hook and the Dermavia "D" at the end. Nothing else is red.

---

## 1. Three candidate structures

**A. Rooftop to skin to shelf (chosen).** One take: a night sky over Baghdad rooftops with the hook carved into it, then dawn is carved open and the sun rises on someone who walked out without sunscreen. The camera follows a ray of light down into the skin, drawn as a printed landscape where the spots are ink pools that spread under the sun. It rises out to a bathroom shelf with the cream, then pulls back over the rooftops in daylight.
*Why:* the hook lands as text in the first second, the cause (sun → melanin) is something we can *see* as ink spreading, and the product comes as the answer, not as the opening.

**B. The same cheek printed again.** A locked frame of one cheek, peeling sheets day by day with and without sunscreen.
*Rejected:* it reads as a before/after promise, which the brand rules forbid, and it wastes the long-take move.

**C. A street at noon, one window lit per hour.** A street as a clock; each hour in the sun darkens one mark.
*Rejected:* too abstract for a 30-second Reel; the product has no natural place in it.

## 2. Logline and arc

**Logline:** Using a brightening cream while going out in the sun without sunscreen darkens spots; the print shows why, and shows the cream and the sunscreen as one routine.

- **Setup (0–8 s):** the mistake, carved into the sky and onto a rooftop.
- **Turn (8–14 s):** inside the skin: sunlight makes the ink pools (melanin) spread.
- **Ending (14–30 s):** silence, then the shelf: the cream, what's in it, sunscreen next to it, a sensitivity test. Back over the city by day; the seal stamps the brand.

## 3. Benchmark

- **Learn:** a nature documentary's single tracking shot (the move never cuts); shin-hanga's layered planes (rooftops / palms / river / sky).
- **Don't learn:** cosmetics ads' smooth gradients, glossy close-ups, and the before/after split screen.

## 6. Cue map

- **Tempo:** 84 BPM in 3/4 (one bar ≈ 2.14 s). 12 bars total.
- **Bars 1–2 (0–4.3 s):** harp on two notes and pizzicato bass. The seal ❌ lands on the downbeat of bar 2.
- **Bars 3–4 (4.3–8.6 s):** bowed vibraphone enters with the sunrise. The board line lands on bar 3, beat 2.
- **Bars 5–6 (8.6–13.6 s):** low strings swell in steps with each light pulse (beat 1 of bars 5 and 6). Tension.
- **13.6–14.0 s:** silence.
- **14.0 s:** one timpani stroke. Then a lighter section: harp and glockenspiel, each ingredient tile on a beat.
- **Bars 10–12 (21–26 s):** full small ensemble with low horn, brief resolution. The seal "D" on a downbeat at 24.0; the strings ring out under the peel.

## 8. Type design

- **Typeface:** the style wants one bold serif. For Arabic: **Noto Naskh Arabic Bold** (OFL). Latin ingredient names: **Noto Serif Bold**. DIN Next / Futura are sans-serif and would break the print look; the brand name appears on the bottle label as it is.
- **Where words live:** the sky plate (hook and closing), a board on a water tank, a carved stone in the skin, small tiles on the shelf. No title cards, no subtitles (no voice).
- **How they appear:** cut letter by letter, right to left for Arabic, with chips at the knife; the seal is pressed.
- **Sizes at 1080 wide:** at most three per frame; hook ~92 px, tiles ~48 px, ingredient names ≥ 34 px. Every word at least 5% inside the frame, kept clear of the Reels UI (bottom ~20% and right ~12%).
- **Reading time:** every line holds at least 1.8 s fully cut. The hook is readable by 1.8 s, inside the 3-second window.

## 9. Compliance (Dermavia rules)

- No "final cure" or "guaranteed result" language; the film shows the cause and the routine, not a before/after.
- No percentages (none are in the catalog for this product).
- Shot with the sunscreen and sensitivity test was removed at Ahmed's request. Both reminders move to the **caption** (required by brand rules): "⚠️ لازم واقي شمس SPF 50+ كل صباح" and "⚠️ سوّي اختبار حساسية على الرسغ قبل أول استعمال".

## 10. Decisions (approved by Ahmed, 2026-11-14)

- **Product:** carved prop inside the take; at the end a sheet peels to the real product photo (`assets/product.jpg`) as a pasted notice. Total length now **26 s**.
- **Shot 5 (sunscreen + sensitivity test) removed**; the reminders live in the caption.
- **Type:** Noto Naskh Arabic Bold + Noto Serif Bold; brand name stays as printed on the pack.
- **Length:** kept as written. If the first render feels rushed in shots 2–3, cut the second light pulse (10.7 s) first.
- **Storyboard:** approved in words. Go straight to style frames → production.
